# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation in UFGU should be constructive, patient, and focused on the work.
Treat other contributors and users with respect regardless of experience,
identity, hardware, language, or technical opinion.

Acceptable participation includes giving reproducible evidence, reviewing ideas
in good faith, acknowledging mistakes, and respecting maintainer boundaries.
Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
misinformation, doxxing, and publication of private material are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its
community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repetition,
and the safety of affected participants.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact available on
the hosting platform. Do not open a public issue containing private evidence.
